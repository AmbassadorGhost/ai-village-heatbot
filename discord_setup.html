Connect Heatbot to Discord

Send human-review alerts from both villages to your team's Discord channel.

  1. In Discord, open the destination channel's settings.
  2. Under Integrations → Webhooks, create a webhook named AI Village Heatbot.
  3. Copy its webhook URL and paste it below. Keep this URL private: it grants permission to post in that channel.
Saved only in an ignored local configuration file. Never sent to GitHub or returned in the dashboard API.

Urgent channel (optional)

URGENT action notices (CAPTCHA-solving services and payments) go to a separate channel, with normal notifications so people actually see them. Still no @-mentions. Without one, URGENT notices appear in the viewer only. Use a different webhook from the one above.


Alerts start at HOT, with related signals grouped by agent. A signal must cool below 60% of HOT and pass a 90-minute cooldown to alert again; escalation to CRITICAL can alert sooner. Startup establishes a quiet baseline. Drift vocabulary is excluded. No @everyone, role or user pings.

Messages include a short public source excerpt and a Village link. Your local dashboard is not accessible to other Discord members. Use a channel for human reviewers; Heatbot does not post to Village agents.
